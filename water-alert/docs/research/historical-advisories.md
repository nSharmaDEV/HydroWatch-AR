## Implementation boundary and future reviews

The curated client-side catalog is a **history-only projection**, not a new live
feed or an update to `data/live`, hosted storage, source snapshots, event journals
or notification subscriptions. `ImportedAdvisory` deliberately is not a live
`Notice`. Only `SupplierAlertHistory` consumes the merged projection. Existing
saved notice filtering and collection-start calculation remain source-based;
imports never backdate collection or clear a current/unavailable status.

Additional entries require readable primary issue evidence, exact supplier
identity, original affected scope, dates with honest precision, review date and
document links. An explicit lift needs its own official evidence; missing or
unreadable lift evidence is unknown, not a lift. Preserve inconsistencies visibly.
If an import represents an already-observed event, add its **reviewed exact
notice IDs** to `observedNoticeIds`; the history projection displays the imported
card instead of repeating those saved rows, without deleting saved evidence.
Never merge events merely because they share a date, city or approximate area.
The Rogers event has no known observed counterpart in the reviewed live archive.
