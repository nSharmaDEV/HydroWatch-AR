Agency guidance

Verbatim from ADEQ’s HAB page — not advice written by this app.

“A water body with no HAB advisory is not an indication that a bloom is not present. Blooms are unpredictable. They can develop rapidly and may move across a water body due to wind or water current. If you suspect that a water body is experiencing a harmful algal bloom, it is best to avoid.”

If you suspect a bloom: keep children and pets away, avoid contact, and report it.

  • Avoid areas of algae accumulation, especially for pets and small children
  • Swimming, wading, skiing and jet skiing are discouraged near visible blooms
  • Avoid direct contact with water, and wash with clean water after any contact
  • Clean fish well with potable water and eat the fillet portion only
  • Contact a medical professional after exposure if nausea, rashes, diarrhea or breathing problems begin

Report a bloom: (501) 682-0744 · ADEQ HAB report form
Possible bloom illness: (501) 280-4168 · adh.ep.its@arkansas.gov

4 · What changed vs today

  • Copy: the “cannot confirm whether active or lifted” sentence becomes a checkable statement (ADEQ's own table omits this row) plus an explicit description of what the app does about it.
  • New fields on the history card: published affected area, feed-updated date, first observed, and an observation count — all already stored, none newly claimed.
  • Agency guidance: the “guidance has not been verified” gap is filled with verbatim ADEQ text, a link and a retrieval date.
  • Unchanged: still not “active”, still not “safe”, still no notification in either direction, still three visually distinct classes.
  • Still open: the official meaning of “N/A” and the asterisk — one email to ee.habs@arkansas.gov closes it.
