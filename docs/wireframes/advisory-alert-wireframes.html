Water Alert — wireframes

Static synthetic design for a resident water-advisory alert app (working name TBD). Not a shipped UI. Every screen exists to protect one rule: the app only ever mirrors what an official agency published, and it never converts the absence of a notice into a claim of safety.
Sources: ADH boil-water notices + ADEQ harmful-algal-bloom advisories. Coverage: statewide, NWA-first.

Active advisory / notice No active advisory found (not proof of safety) Data unavailable Sample (demo) data Official source record
Flow: Set your water→ Home / status→ (a change is detected)→ Push notification→ Advisory detail + next steps→ share / track-lift→ History
Three honest states, always. Active · No active advisory found · Data unavailable. A blank "date lifted" means the notice is still active. Vague or partial-area notices are shown as published, never narrowed to an address. If a source fails, the app says so and shows agency contacts — it never silently shows "all clear."

What we don't claim
We do not say water is safe. No notice is not proof of safety. We do not predict blooms. We do not test water, detect toxins or species, or give medical advice. We are not affiliated with ADH or ADEQ.
Your data
We store your chosen place and, if you opt in, one contact to alert. History stays on your device. Delete it any time from Alerts & privacy. Nothing is published or sold.
Matching rule: a record is shown for you only when the published system/lake name (or a documented alias, or a partial area that names your county) matches your choice. Partial-area notices are shown as written, never widened to an address.
If a source changes shape or goes down, the app shows "Data unavailable" and names the source — it never guesses.
