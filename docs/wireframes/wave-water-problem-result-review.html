    Design review notes

    Selected resources, not a directory dump

    The screen-2 answer selects the contact role and checklist. Operational results do not include ADH quality resources; quality results do not assume a supplier email is the destination. Both explains two distinct roles.

    Not sure explains both roles and offers a choice without diagnosing or silently classifying the concern. No AG, legal, annual-report, receipts, or compensation resources are added by default.

    Missing contact stays missing

    The Leisure Hills contact comes from its exact supplier-ID record. The synthetic unknown supplier receives bill/park-management guidance, never Leisure Hills’ details or a nearby supplier’s number.

    A supplier directory entry is a general contact, not a verified billing department or a promise of service. An unknown supplier does not remove the general ADH quality resource.

    Evidence, not fresh verification

    Supplier fields retain the October 14, 2026 check date. The existing guide records the ADH program-contact check on October 18, 2026. Neither was reverified for this design review. Checked does not mean staffed now or accepted for this issue.

    No live notice data is loaded. No current advisory, affected address, lifting status, or safety conclusion is asserted.
