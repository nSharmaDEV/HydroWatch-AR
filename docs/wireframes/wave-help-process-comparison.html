The design question

Can a resident get immediate guidance, send a properly sourced request to the right organization, and keep an honest record of what happens next?

Wireframe direction & boundaries

White panels, navy text, teal controls, gray outlines and amber advisory notices. System sans-serif; left-aligned reading. The side-by-side comparison is the focus, not new branding.

Existing notice citations are retained. Historical report findings are a proposed opt-in addition, not proof of current noncompliance. Regulatory rule citations remain undecided.

No universal advisory duration, flushing recipe or compensation entitlement. Follow official local instructions. The tracker is updated by the resident, not by an agency feed.
