Real-world process vs. proposed WellPower help

Illustrative task comparison, not a measured user study. People may already have these contacts or resources.

Without WellPower

  • Check the official notice and identify which instructions apply.
  • Find a provider contact on the bill or directory; find the relevant agency or assistance resource if needed.
  • Write down dates, contact attempts, actual costs, and the requested response.
  • Contact the provider or complete an external organization’s process, then keep copies and follow up.

With the proposed guided flow

  • Start from the selected supplier; see official notice context when verified and available.
  • Find scoped guidance and existing checked resource destinations in one place.
  • Use a checklist and editable template; review the facts and copy or download the preparation.
  • Still make the call, send the email, or complete the external form yourself. Keep your own confirmation and follow up.

The proposed benefit is organization and preparation—not proven time savings, automatic filing, faster agency action, guaranteed jurisdiction, or reimbursement.

Source boundaries to preserve

Existing repository references, not newly verified in this review. No live feed is read and no current advisory is asserted here.

  • Official ADH notice list: https://health.arkansas.gov/wa_engTraining/boilwaterorder.aspx. Failed, stale, or historical data must never appear as confirmed current status.
  • General CDC guidance: https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/drinking-water-advisories-an-overview.html. Existing excerpts checked Oct 14, 2026; general boil-water reference only, not incident-specific ADH instructions or chemical/do-not-drink/do-not-use advice.
  • ADH Safe Water Engineering contact source: https://healthy.arkansas.gov/programs-services/licensing-military-member-licensure-permits-plan-reviews/drinking-water-plan-review. Existing guide records its contact check on Oct 18, 2026; not an emergency-response service.
  • Consumer complaint destination: https://arkansasag.gov/file-a-complaint/. Review official scope and instructions; not an outage or water-quality reporting service. Complaints may be shared with the business and become public records. No jurisdiction, mediation, reimbursement, or response-time guarantee.
  • Legal information: https://www.arlawhelp.org/, with the existing legal-aid intake and small-claims references retained for relevant later screens. Eligibility, availability, and representation are not guaranteed. No legal advice.

An advisory alone proves neither personal loss nor liability. Keep sensitive account, medical, and identity information out of drafts. Drafts are not durable records. Any later implementation must preserve these limits at the relevant action, not only in this review note.
