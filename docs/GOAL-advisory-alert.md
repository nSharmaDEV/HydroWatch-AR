# WellPower: WAVE — product direction

The sole active product is **WellPower: WAVE**, an Arkansas resident app for
published water advisories, supplier information and preparation tools.

Start with the [project overview](../README.md) and
[app documentation](../water-alert/README.md). The
[detailed implementation notes](../water-alert/IMPLEMENTATION-NOTES.md),
[changelog](../water-alert/CHANGELOG.md) and
[push verification gates](../water-alert/PUSH-PLAN.md) distinguish implemented,
locally tested and deployed behavior.

The two live advisory sources remain ADH boil-water notices and ADEQ HAB
advisories. Identity references, historical annual reports and general guidance
are separate from current incident status. Failed/stale feeds are unavailable,
not all-clear; no advisory found never establishes safety. Samples are fictional.

Older design documents are historical proposals, not additional products or
proof of shipped features. Email alerts, water testing and prediction are not
implemented. Automatic provider/agency submissions are not performed.
